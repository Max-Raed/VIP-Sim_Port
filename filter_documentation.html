VipSim filter documentation

Generated from the tuner's built-in explanations. The same texts appear as tooltips in the tuner.

The order of the steps matters. Filters run top to bottom, and each step works on the result of the one before it. Example: a scotoma placed BEFORE a fog step gets softened by the fog; placed AFTER it, the scotoma stays crisp on top. Blur before floaters keeps the dots sharp; blur after floaters blurs the dots too. Reorder steps in the tuner by dragging their handle.

bcg

Changes overall lightness and how strong the colors are.

brightness: 1 = unchanged, 1.5 = much brighter, 2.5+ = heavy white-out. contrast: 1 = unchanged, 0.5 = pale, 0 = flat grey. saturation: 1 = normal colors, 0 = black-and-white. gamma: below 1 lifts dark areas, above 1 darkens them.

parameterdefaultmeaning
brightness1.0Overall lightness: 1 = unchanged, above 1 = brighter.
contrast1.0Contrast: 1 = unchanged. LOW values also wash colors toward grey.
gamma1.0Midtones: below 1 lifts dark areas, above 1 darkens them.
saturation1.0Color amount: 1 = normal, 0 = black-and-white.
color_space'linear'linear = faithful to the Unity shader, srgb = simpler math directly on screen colors. Choices: linear, srgb.
